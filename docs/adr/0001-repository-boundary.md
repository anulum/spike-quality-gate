# Decision: separate source admission, measurements and policy

## Context

Event presence alone cannot identify capture coverage, adequate exposure or upstream loss. A parser pass and an attractive scalar can conceal missing source facts or an incomplete population.

## Decision

Spike Quality Gate retains the full prior population, source-specific measurements, immutable typed policy and all located report details. Parsing, conversion, capture, shared evidence, storage and downstream acceptance remain with their actual owners.

## Consequences

Repository controls can be qualified before a domain engine exists. Unknowns and known failures remain visible together. Comparison cannot merge policies, attribution cannot subtract defects and a recorded benchmark exception cannot turn failed data into a passing precondition. No additional parser, recorder or evidence service is introduced.
