# Contributor conduct

Contributors treat people respectfully, attribute sources accurately and report technical disagreements with reproducible evidence. Harassment, discrimination, threats and disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to Miroslav Šotek at protoscience@anulum.li. Include only information needed to assess the concern and avoid distributing private correspondence. The maintainer may request correction, restrict participation or remove harmful content. No response-time commitment is advertised.
